**Nothing is `manually_validated`**

## The keep rule

A masked item survives only if the human review recorded **all** of:

1. `masked_variant_valid = yes` — tried, could not answer
2. `reviewer_gold_label` ∈ that item's `noncommit_labels`
3. `evidence_sufficient_for_gold = no`
4. `keep_or_exclude = keep`
5. no reconstructibility indication in `notes`

`unclear` on rule 1 excludes: an unconfirmed suspicion of a leak is treated as a leak, because a masked item with a wrong gold inverts the overcommitment metric and nothing downstream catches it.

## Leak routes in the excluded items

The human review recorded **no flags** — the review app offered them but the reviewer used the structured fields instead. The routes below come from the two AI reviewers' flags on the same items, and are corroborating rather than primary evidence.

| flag | occurrences |
|---|---|
| `answer_reconstructible` | 60 |
| `total_minus_components` | 11 |
| `direction_stated_in_prose` | 2 |

`total_minus_components` is a route `masking.py` already guards, and the automated check passed every one of these items. Its appearance here means the guard's coverage is narrower than the route: it tests pipe-delimited table columns, and misses the same arithmetic when the figures sit in prose, across two tables, or behind one more step. **That is the concrete guard gap to fix before constructing more masks.**

## Two things worth a second look in the KEPT set

- **10 kept item(s) were flagged `evidence_gutted`** by an AI reviewer. A gutted document is a different defect from a leak: a model may decline because the input looks damaged rather than because the evidence is absent, which confounds the very measurement these items exist for. They pass the human rule and are kept, but they should be read.

  `hqa_CFQ_1279a468_masked`, `hqa_CFQ_446ed3a5_masked`, `hqa_CFQ_6e99706b_masked`, `hqa_CFQ_7897a7cc_masked`, `hqa_CFQ_7f731a74_masked`, `hqa_FB_5dbbcec0_masked`, `hqa_FB_9fc58fab_masked`, `hqa_FB_d397e71d_masked`, `hqa_TAT_5ce27434_masked`, `hqa_TAT_924db34b_masked`

## What the cleanup costs

Non-committal gold: **66/319 = 20.7%** (was 112/368 = 30.4%).
Of these, **49 are constructed** masked items and **17 are natural**, all in FinTradeBench.

Removing half the masked variants removes half the constructed non-committal stratum. Overcommitment and wrong-non-committal-type are now measurable on a smaller base, and outside FinTradeBench they still rest entirely on constructed items — just fewer, better ones. That is the honest trade: the metric is narrower and the items behind it are trustworthy.
