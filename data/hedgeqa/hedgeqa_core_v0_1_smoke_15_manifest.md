## Caveats

- 15 items cannot support a rate. Use these to check the pipeline runs, parses, and scores — not to measure hedging.
- The main manifest deliberately avoids the hardest items, so a clean pass on it says nothing about the edge set.
- Both manifests are subsets of the validated collection, whose masked items are constructed and whose non-committal stratum outside FinTradeBench is entirely constructed.
